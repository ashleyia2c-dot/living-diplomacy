[h1]Living Diplomacy: talk to the leaders of WARHAMMER III[/h1]
Write to any faction leader in your own words, right inside the diplomacy screen, and they answer you in character. Greet them, bargain, flatter, threaten, or just talk.

[list]
[*]All [b]118 legendary lords[/b] have their own lore and personality.
[*]Leaders weigh the balance of power and their culture: a weak realm flatters you, a strong one dictates terms, Chaos cares for no one.
[*]Treacherous factions (Skaven, Dark Elves, Tzeentch...) can sign a treaty with you, drop hints, and then betray you in the middle of a letter.
[*]Deals you accept, like non aggression pacts, are really signed in your campaign.
[*]Leaders write letters to you on their own, and remember what you promised them.
[*]Works in all 13 languages of the game. Completely free.
[/list]

[h1]New in 0.43: the courts come alive[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]A living world.[/b] Every turn the courts of the factions you follow decide what to do: seek allies, make peace, sign pacts, or go to war with a rival. Up to two of those plans really happen in your campaign each turn, checked against the balance of power, and never against you or your allies. You can switch this off at any time.
[*][b]Court rumours.[/b] The courts spread gossip about each other's rulers: love affairs, suspected betrayals, envy, ambition, bootlicking. Rumours travel between the courts that would hear them, and the rulers react to what they heard. Some of them are lies.
[*][b]Your own court.[/b] At the start of each turn a member of your court tells you what matters: who is plotting, who is suffering, how your wars are going. You can talk to your court and ask for advice.
[*][b]The World tab[/b] with the chronicle of the campaign, the rumours and the deeds of the courts.
[*][b]Leaders know who is who.[/b] Mention a faction, a ruler, a god or a famous place and the leader knows what you are talking about, in any language.
[*][b]Long memory.[/b] Leaders remember your promises, your gifts and how you treated them, even many turns later.
[*][b]The companion now updates with the mod.[/b] Download it once more for 0.43 and from then on Steam keeps it up to date together with the mod.
[/list]

[h1]READ THIS FIRST: the mod has two parts[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]The mod[/b]. Steam installs it for you when you press Subscribe, and keeps it updated. It adds the chat to the diplomacy screen.
[*][b]The companion[/b]. A small free program that lets the leaders actually answer. Steam cannot install programs, only mod files, so you download it once from the link below. From 0.43 on, it updates itself through the mod.
[/olist]
[b]Why?[/b] WARHAMMER III does not allow mods to connect to anything outside the game. Every AI mod needs a small program running next to the game to do that part.

[b]If you only subscribe[/b], you will see the chat, but the leaders will not answer. The game tells you when the companion is missing, so you will not be left guessing.

[h1]How to play[/h1]
Open diplomacy with any faction and click the [b]small hand icon next to the question mark[/b]. Type your message at the bottom and send it. The leader usually answers within a few seconds.

The [b]AI Diplomacy[/b] button opens the Living Diplomacy window: [b]Letters[/b] from other rulers, [b]Who can write to me[/b] to choose which factions may write to you and take part in the living world, and [b]World[/b] for your court's report, the chronicle and the rumours. The icon beside your court's report lets you talk to your court.

[h1]"Windows protected your PC"[/h1]
The companion is made by a single indie developer and is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows does not recognise the publisher the first time you open it. Click [b]More info[/b] and then [b]Run anyway[/b]. The full source code is public on [url=https://github.com/ashleyia2c-dot/living-diplomacy]GitHub[/url].

If your antivirus blocks it and you prefer not to allow it, use the alternative below.

[h1]Something not working?[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]The chat says "The Player2 companion is not answering"[/b]: the companion window is not open. Open WH3-LLM-Diplomacy-Companion.exe, leave it open, and send your message again.
[*][b]The companion window is open but nobody answers[/b]: check that Player2 is open and signed in, and read the message in the companion window.
[*][b]The companion asks you to download a newer version[/b]: this is rare, only when an update needs it. Download it again from the link above.
[*][b]The companion cannot find your game[/b]: this happens when WARHAMMER III is on another drive. The README inside the ZIP explains the one line fix.
[*][b]Two copies of the mod[/b]: keep only this Steam version enabled in the launcher. Do not copy another .pack file into the game's data folder.
[/list]

[h1]Questions[/h1]
[b]Does it cost anything?[/b] No. The mod, the companion and Player2 are free.

[b]Why is subscribing not enough?[/b] Steam can only install mod files, and the game does not let mods reach the AI by themselves. The companion is the small bridge between the two.

[b]Can the AI factions declare war on me by themselves?[/b] No. The courts can only make treaties and wars between AI factions, and never against your allies. Anything that concerns you arrives as a letter that you can refuse. Switch [b]AI treaties[/b] off in the World tab if you prefer a quieter world.

[b]Is my data private?[/b] The companion only talks to the Player2 app on your own computer. Your conversations are saved on your computer, in your user folder (AppData\Roaming\LivingDiplomacy).

[b]Multiplayer?[/b] No, single-player campaigns only.

Bug reports are welcome in the comments: tell us what you did, which factions were involved, and copy any message from the companion window.
